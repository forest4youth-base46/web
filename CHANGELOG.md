## v1.3 · Branded exports
- Every PDF, PNG and print carries the project logo (at the branding
  guide's A4 minimum) and a disclaimer: a note for that kind of document,
  plus the funding statement. Forest and IVN tools get Download PDF and a
  branded Print; `.txt` downloads removed; a plain browser print is
  branded too.
- Header: the Forest Interventions / Immersive Virtual Nature switch
  replaces Practitioner / Exploring.
- Walk the Forest: the lower rail's segments walk to their activity again.
- FR/DE translation of the guides and tools.
- Version shown in export footers.

## v1.2 · Walk the Forest 3D
- The 3D walk (`?wf=3d`): living stations with 20–30 s loops, arrival
  moments, idle walk, the IVN room as an 18th stop past the campfire and
  a fallen log, the SAM+ door with its project plaque; header logo.

## v1.1 · Practical Guides
- The two WP1 practical guides (Forest-Based Interventions, Immersive
  Virtual Nature) as readable sections; the Forest and IVN tool hubs;
  the opt-in WebGL backdrop.

## v1.0 · Live
- The public site: Pocketbook of activities, Session Builder, Run Mode,
  Reflect, Reference, the painted Walk the Forest backdrop.
